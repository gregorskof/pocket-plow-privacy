Pocket Plow Demo

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 18, 2026

Pocket Plow Demo does not collect, store, or share any personal information from players.

Information We Collect

Pocket Plow Demo does not require an account and does not collect personal information from players.

The game does not collect:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Location data
  • Contacts
  • Photos or media files
  • Camera or microphone data
  • Personal identifiers

Ads, Analytics, and Online Services

Pocket Plow Demo does not use analytics, does not show ads, and does not use online tracking services.

Local Game Progress

The game may save simple gameplay progress locally on the player’s device. This can include things such as:

  • Coins
  • Unlocked vehicles
  • Selected vehicle
  • Level progress
  • Game settings

This information is stored only on the player’s device and is not sent to the developer or any third party.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
